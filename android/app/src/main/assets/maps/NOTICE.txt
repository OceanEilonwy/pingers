Map assets bundled with the app (android/docs/maps.md):

- style.json: generated from @protomaps/basemaps 5.7.2 (BSD-3-Clause), "light" flavour, recoloured.
- glyphs/: Noto Sans glyph ranges from protomaps/basemaps-assets, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (glyphs/OFL.txt).
- sprites/: v4 sprites from protomaps/basemaps-assets, derived from tangrams/icons (MIT).

Map data shown with them is OpenStreetMap (ODbL): "© OpenStreetMap contributors".
