Privacy

How Pingers keeps you private

Pingers is built so that the people running it can't see where you are, who you meet, or what you say. Here's how, and where the limits are.

Your Party is locked to its members

Party names, members and Spots are encrypted on your phone. Only members' phones hold the keys. The safety code you compare when joining proves nobody slipped in between you.

Finding friends without telling the server

Your phone posts anonymous check-ins that only your Party can recognise, and they look different every time. Your phone, not the server, spots a friend in the same Spot, and only for the Spot you're in.

Waking a friend's phone

The alert goes through a separate push relay. The server knows an alert was sent but not to which phone. The relay knows a phone was woken but not why or by whom. Turn on "No wake-up pushes" and nobody sees it at all.

Hiding your internet address

Everything anonymous, including live locations in a meetup, travels through a privacy relay run by someone else. The relay sees your address but not what you send; the server sees what arrives but not from where.

Meetups are just for who was there

Each meetup gets brand-new keys shared only by the people in it. Party members who weren't there can't read it, people who join later don't see earlier messages, and when everyone leaves, every phone deletes the chat and keys.

Your exact spot is yours to share

In a meetup, others see you're there, not where. Your position is locked with your own key, which you hand only to people you approve. Others can't even tell whether you've shared with anyone.

Pauses that look like nothing

Focus, Do Not Disturb and your exclusion zones are checked on your phone and never leave it. While paused, your phone keeps sending blank check-ins, so being paused looks exactly like being somewhere else.

No phone number, no tracking ID

Your account is a key made on your phone. To stop spam without knowing who you are, your phone proves "I'm a real member" mathematically, without saying which member. Nothing is kept as a location history.

What Pingers can't protect

Party members you trust. Any member could claim to be at a Spot, or use a modified app to count how many people are at one. Only invite people you'd meet anyway.
People you share with. Someone you approve can screenshot where you were. Stopping sharing locks them out from then on.
Operators working together. If the server and the push relay pooled their logs, they could work out who met whom, but still not where.
Apple and Google. They see that a notification arrived, not what it says. "No wake-up pushes" stops this.
An unlocked, stolen phone. It reveals your Parties, Spots and exclusion zones. Ask a member to remove the lost phone.
